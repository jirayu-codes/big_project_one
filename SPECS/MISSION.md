# PlantPal — Mission

## Purpose

PlantPal helps people stop forgetting when to water, fertilize, or repot their
plants. Each plant has different needs, and it is easy to lose track when you
have more than a couple.

## Who It Is For

Anyone who owns houseplants or garden plants and wants to keep track of their
care needs.

## What Success Looks Like

You can add a plant and see when it is next due to be watered.

## Non-Negotiables

Out of scope on purpose. We will not add these:

- No sharing or exporting data.
- No user accounts or login — it's a single-user app.
- No care history log — only the last watered date is tracked.
